CareBridge – Smart Healthcare & Hospital Management System

Project Overview

CareBridge is a smart healthcare management platform designed to connect patients, hospitals, and doctors through a single digital system. It simplifies hospital appointment booking, patient management, and healthcare access while improving communication between patients and hospitals.

How It Works

1. Patient Portal

Patients can create an account and securely log in using their email and password.

Search for nearby hospitals, clinics, and doctors.

Book appointments with their preferred hospital and doctor.

Use an AI-powered Symptoms Checker to get preliminary health guidance based on reported symptoms and uploaded images.

View appointments, manage bookings, and access medical records.

Receive appointment updates and notifications.

2. Hospital Portal

Hospitals can create accounts and securely log in.

Automatically access their registered hospital profile.

View all patient appointments booked with their hospital.

Access patient and appointment details.

Manage appointments, update booking statuses, and maintain patient records.

Receive notifications about new bookings and appointment updates.

3. Appointment Synchronization

When a patient books an appointment, the booking is saved in the database.

The appointment automatically appears in the corresponding hospital dashboard.

Hospitals can manage the booking and update its status.

Patients can view the latest appointment status from their portal.

4. AI-Based Symptoms Checker

Patients can upload an image and describe their symptoms.

The AI system analyses the submitted information.

It provides possible health conditions, general guidance, and suitable medical specialists.

It helps patients understand when they should seek professional medical attention. It does not replace a doctor's diagnosis.

5. Nearby Hospitals

Uses the patient's current location with permission.

Finds nearby hospitals and clinics.

Displays available location details and distances.

Helps patients find suitable healthcare facilities.

Main Objective

The main objective of CareBridge is to make healthcare services more accessible, simplify hospital operations, reduce manual appointment management, and create a seamless connection between patients and hospitals.

Key Technologies

Frontend: React, HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Backend: Node.js, Express.js

Database: Connected application database

Authentication: Email and Password Authentication

AI Integration: AI-powered symptom analysis

Location Services: GPS and Maps Integration

Project Outcome

CareBridge provides an integrated digital healthcare experience where patients can find healthcare services and book appointments, while hospitals can efficiently manage patient bookings and hospital operations through a centralized platform.
